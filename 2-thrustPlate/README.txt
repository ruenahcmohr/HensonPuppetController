2nd part, this is an easy one. 
simple print, make sure the spline shaft from a potentiometer 
fits firmly into the hole, it should print a bit small and 
need to be reamed out just a bit with a 6mm or 15/64" drill bit.
This screws into the top of the mounting pin, its designed for 
M3 machine screws. 6/32 screws or short #6 wood scrwes should also work ok.
There is no special alignment to worry about on this part.
